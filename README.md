# The Commonwealth Group

**Onshore mortgage fulfillment, compliance consulting and technology for lenders.**
Website: https://www.thecommonwealth.net

## Overview

The Commonwealth Group (CWG) is a U.S.-based provider of contract services, regulatory consulting and mortgage technology for lenders. Established in 2008 in Memphis, Tennessee, it began as a fair lending and compliance consultancy and has grown into a single onshore partner across operations, compliance and technology.

Its core pitch: lenders can scale with volume, up for a refi wave and down when it passes, without carrying fixed headcount through a market downturn. Clients can take the whole stack or just the piece they are short on, with no required bundle.

## What They Do

### Contract Services
- **Contract Underwriting**: CRU-accredited underwriters covering QM, Non-QM, construction and HELOC. Two business day turn times, with no volume minimum on standard QM.
- **Contract Processing**: Full-file or overflow processing, working inside the client's LOS like an internal processor.
- **Quality Control**: Pre- and post-close audits, targeted reviews and defect-trending reports.
- **CondoAnalytics™**: A Commonwealth Group company with its own team and platform, performing full and limited condo project reviews with eligibility findings and supporting documentation ([condoanalytics.com](https://www.condoanalytics.com)).

### Consulting & Compliance
- Regulatory compliance and fair lending
- HMDA data scrubs, geocoding validation and filing support
- Mortgage Call Report (MCR) preparation and filing, reconciled to the LOS and NMLS record
- Operational consulting (workflow redesign, capacity modeling, cost-per-loan analysis)
- Expert witness services (underwriting standards, servicing conduct, compliance practice)
- Portfolio due diligence (pre-purchase and pre-securitization file reviews)

### AI & Technology
- **CLEAR**: AI-powered mortgage income calculation and automation
- **Encompass & LOS Admin Services**: Certified administrators managing configuration, business rules, input forms and release testing
- **Cybersecurity**: FNMA and FHLMC agency-aligned security assessments and attestation support
- **Penetration Testing**: External and internal testing with prioritized remediation guidance

## Why Lenders Choose Them

1. **No minimums** for QM underwriting: send one file or one hundred.
2. **100% onshore**: every underwriter, processor and auditor is U.S.-based.
3. **Compliance in the DNA**: the company grew out of fair lending and compliance work, so the work is done by people who know how it will be examined.
4. **Elastic in both directions**: scale up and down without severance, ramp time or idle salary.

## How Engagements Start

1. **Scoping call** with a practitioner to map products, volumes and pain points.
2. **Scope & setup**: load the client's overlays and templates and connect to their LOS, or define a consulting scope.
3. **Pilot, then scale**: begin with a batch of files, a single audit or one system, then expand or dial back.

No long-term lock-in or enterprise procurement cycle is required.
